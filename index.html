From Vague Guideline to Consistent Rubric

I turn vague guidelines into criteria that two careful reviewers apply the same way.

Raters often get one line of guidance. I took such a line, wrote seven responses that split careful raters, and built the guideline, rubric, edge-case map, and calibration examples that give each response exactly one score.

Guideline writing, rubric design, edge-case analysis, rater calibration, multilingual evaluation (German, Swedish)

Before: the guidance

Rate how well the response follows the user’s instructions, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).

After: the rubric, in short
  • 5 Fully follows
  • 4 Follows, minor flaw
  • 3 Partly follows
  • 2 Mostly misses
  • 1 Does not follow
